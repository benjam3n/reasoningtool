Does the impossibility of a clean definition strengthen the concept or weaken it?

If domination is undefinable, is it unresistable?

Or is the indefinability of domination evidence of its pervasiveness?

Is the pervasiveness of domination evidence for its existence or evidence of conceptual inflation?

Can you distinguish between a concept that is pervasive because the phenomenon is pervasive and a concept that is pervasive because the thinker is obsessed?

Is obsession with domination a symptom of domination or a response to it?

Can the two be distinguished?

If they cannot be distinguished, does the question collapse?

Does the collapse of the question constitute the victory of domination or the liberation from the concept?

---

Is the study of domination itself a power relation?

Does the theorist of domination occupy a position of authority over the subject?

If the theorist names domination, does the naming grant power?

Does the power to name domination reproduce what it critiques?

Can critique escape the logic of the thing it critiques?

If critique cannot escape, is all critical theory trapped in the very structures it describes?

Is being trapped the condition of thought under domination?

If thought is trapped, can trapped thought still be true?

Can truth exist within domination?

If truth can exist within domination, does domination have limits?

If domination has limits, are the limits found in truth?

---

Does the interrogation of domination ever reach a foundation?

Or does every answer generate another question?

If every answer generates another question, is the interrogation infinite?

Is an infinite interrogation a form of freedom or a form of imprisonment?

If you are imprisoned in an infinite regress of questions, who imprisoned you?

Did the questions imprison you or did the domination that provoked them?

Is the provocation of endless questioning the last form domination takes?

Or is endless questioning the first form freedom takes?

Can you tell the difference?

Does the inability to tell the difference matter?

If it does not matter, have you finally escaped domination?

If it does matter, are you still within it?

Is the uncertainty itself the answer?

Is the answer itself another question?

Does domination end where the questions end, or do the questions end where domination ends?

Or do neither end?
