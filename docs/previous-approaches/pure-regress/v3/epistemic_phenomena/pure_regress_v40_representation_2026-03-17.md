# Recursive Causal Interrogation: Representation

WHAT THIS CONCEPT COVERS:
What makes something a representation of something else. Whether representation requires resemblance. Mental representation vs. linguistic representation vs. pictorial representation. Whether all thought is representational. The gap between representation and reality. Whether representation distorts what it represents. Misrepresentation.
